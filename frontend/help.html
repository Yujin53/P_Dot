P_Dot Help & Support Center

Have questions about rides, fares, or driver verification? We're here to assist you.

How does P_Dot calculate fares?

Fares are based on configurable local Tuguegarao guidelines: a base fare covering the first kilometer, a modest succeeding distance charge, and slight traffic duration allowances. All prices are calculated in Philippine Pesos (PHP).

How does driver verification work?

Before accepting any ride on P_Dot, every driver must submit their professional/non-professional driver's license, government identification, and vehicle OR/CR documentation. Administrators manually review each credential before granting active status.
