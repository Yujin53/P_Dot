About P_Dot Tuguegarao

The Local Solution for Cagayan's Capital

Tuguegarao City is the vibrant educational, commercial, and governmental center of Region II (Cagayan Valley). However, everyday commuters — from university students commuting between SPUP, USLT, and CSU Carig, to patients visiting Cagayan Valley Medical Center — face persistent transport uncertainty.

National ride-hailing conglomerates like Grab or JoyRide have traditionally prioritized mega-metropolitan centers like Metro Manila and Metro Cebu. This leaves Tuguegarao commuters relying on informal roadside hailing, inconsistent fare negotiations, and limited night-time availability.

P_Dot was created specifically for Tuguegarao to provide an authentic, local-first transport management and ride-hailing service that empowers passengers with clear fares and connects verified local tricycle, motorcycle, and vehicle drivers with reliable passenger demand.

Prototype & Local MVP Notice

P_Dot is currently operated as a functional local web prototype/MVP. Future phases will integrate automated SMS gateways, verified payment switchboards, and live city zoning integrations in accordance with local transport ordinances.
