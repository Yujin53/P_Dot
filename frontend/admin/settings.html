Access Control Policy

In accordance with platform safety requirements:

  • Administrators have full operational authority to inspect drivers, approve vehicles, and reassign or cancel trips.
  • The primary SuperAdmin account cannot be deleted or disabled to guarantee system survivability.
  • All administrative changes, status overrides, and broadcasts are permanently written to the MongoDB Audit Trail.
