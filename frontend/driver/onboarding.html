Local Transport Prototype Policy

P_Dot connects local drivers in Tuguegarao City (Centro, Carig, Caggay, Buntun, Capatan, Pengue-Ruyu, San Gabriel, Ugac, and surrounding barangays). In compliance with local guidelines, drivers must possess valid Philippine driver's licenses and verified vehicle registrations. Once reviewed by our operations team, you will receive an in-app notification enabling you to toggle your status ONLINE to receive ride requests.
